Website

Rodeo Swing Maine

A fast, welcoming website for a weekly country swing dance night in Portland, Maine — built to turn local searches into people on the dance floor.

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Schema.org Local SEO

Project Overview

Rodeo Swing Maine runs a weekly country swing dance class and social in Portland. The goal of the site was simple: help a first-timer find the event, understand exactly what to expect, and feel comfortable walking in the door on a Tuesday night.

The result is a single-page site with a warm, western-inspired look, clear answers to the questions beginners actually ask, and the technical groundwork to show up in local search.

Features

  • Beginner-first content — what a typical night looks like, whether you need a partner, pricing, and dance floor etiquette, all answered up front
  • Photo gallery hero — real photos from the dance floor with a responsive layout tuned for phones
  • TikTok video embeds — recent clips from the floor, lazy-loaded so they never slow the page down
  • Email list signup — Kit (ConvertKit) form restyled to match the site
  • Shareable flyer — monthly flyer with one-tap download for sharing with friends
  • Directions & social links — one-tap maps, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Linktree
  • Looping video footer — loads only when scrolled near and respects reduced-motion settings

Search & Discoverability

The site is built to be found by people searching for country dancing in southern Maine and New Hampshire. It includes complete Open Graph and Twitter card metadata, plus three Schema.org structured data blocks — a recurring DanceEvent with schedule and pricing, a LocalBusiness listing, and a FAQPage — so search engines can surface the key details directly in results.

Design Philosophy

A deep teal, sand, and sienna palette with Rye and Cabin Sketch typography gives the site a friendly honky-tonk feel without getting kitschy. Rope-style dividers separate sections, and generous spacing keeps the page easy to scan on a phone, which is where most visitors will first see it.

Technical Implementation

  • Front end: Hand-written semantic HTML5, CSS3 with custom properties, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks or build step
  • Performance: Lazy-loaded images, iframes, and video; IntersectionObserver for the footer video
  • Accessibility: ARIA labels, keyboard-accessible mobile navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion support
  • SEO: Schema.org JSON-LD, canonical URL, Open Graph, Twitter cards
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 with custom events for directions, social, and TikTok clicks
  • Integrations: Kit email forms, TikTok player embeds, Google Maps links
