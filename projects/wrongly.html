Wrongly

A daily journal for catching your own bad judgment sooner. One sentence a day about what you were wrong about — and a map of your blind spots over time.

SwiftUI Jetpack Compose Core Data Room MVVM

Project Overview

Wrongly is a minimalist daily journaling app built on a simple premise: most people update their beliefs years too late, only after reality forces the correction. Wrongly trains that instinct on purpose, by asking one question every evening — what were you wrong about today?

No goals, no habit tracking, no highlight reel. Just a single honest sentence a day, logged in seconds, that compounds into a real map of where your judgment slips.

The Idea

  • One sentence, not an essay — a guess, an assumption, a read on someone that turned out wrong
  • Ten seconds, no friction — open the app, write, done
  • Compounds over time — a month of entries starts to reveal exactly where overconfidence or excess caution shows up
  • Reframes the habit — admitting you were wrong isn't a failure to log, it's the whole point

Core Features

  • Calendar-based daily log with at-a-glance streak view
  • Multiple entries per day supported
  • Word-frequency insights that surface recurring patterns in what you get wrong
  • Optional daily reminder notification
  • Fully private — entries never leave the device
  • Dark, minimalist “late-evening” interface built around a violet accent
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads

Design Philosophy

The visual language leans into a near-black, low-glare palette with a single glowing violet accent and a champagne-gold reserved for moments that feel like an earned achievement — streaks, milestones, the weekly recap. Everything else stays quiet so the one thing that matters, the day's entry, has nowhere to hide.

Technical Implementation

  • iOS: SwiftUI, Core Data
  • Android: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore
  • Architecture: MVVM
  • Notifications: UserNotifications (iOS), AlarmManager (Android)
  • Privacy: On-device storage only, no account required, no analytics
